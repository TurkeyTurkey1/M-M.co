  • Jog and pivot on whistle (2 min)
  • Freeze frame footwork game (5 min)
  • Partner passing while moving (3 min)

      Tactical Drills Ideas

      Attacking triangle

      Players create a triangle around the shooting circle to give multiple passing options. Progress from uncontested to adding defenders.

      Reset pass

      If stuck, pass back to WA or C to reset and open up a new scoring option.

      Defending the goal circle

      Defenders work together to block the circle. Remember you can jump straight up to defend — not forward.

      Transition drill

      Win the ball and move it quickly out of defence into an attacking formation.

      Technical Drills Ideas

      Passing circle

      Pass and move around the circle. Focus on a loud voice, clear cues and enough force to avoid interceptions.

      Train tracks

      Two lines of attackers and defenders move the ball down the court, increasing the defender ratio as the drill progresses.

      Freeze frame footwork

      Call freeze mid-drill and check the landing foot and balance before the next pass.

      Shooting practice

      GS and GA shoot from inside the circle, focusing on landing, balance and follow-through.

      Cloud Sync

      Connect the site to your Google Sheet so saved plans, setups, checklists, stats and drills update on every device.

      Google Sheets Connection

      The connection URL is already set up for everyone. You only need to change it here if the Google Apps Script is redeployed with a new URL.

      Setup Steps

      1. Open your Google Sheet and choose Extensions > Apps Script.
      2. Paste the contents of google-apps-script.gs into Code.gs.
      3. Click Save, then Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
      4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
      5. Copy the Web app URL and paste it above.

      The script creates a worksheet called WebsiteData in your Sheet. Your existing tabs are untouched. Anyone with the website link can edit shared data; if two people save at once, the last save wins.
