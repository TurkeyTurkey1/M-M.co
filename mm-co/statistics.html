Netball Stat Guide

Shooting

  • Goals — shots that went in
  • Goal Attempts — every shot taken
  • Shooting % — goals ÷ attempts
  • Goal Assists — pass that directly led to a goal

Attack

  • Centre Passes — centre passes taken
  • Feeds — passes into the shooting circle
  • Passes — total passes made
  • Turnovers — possessions lost

Defence

  • Intercepts — balls caught from the opposition
  • Deflections — balls touched but not caught
  • Rebounds — recovered missed shots

Discipline

  • Penalties — total penalties conceded
  • Offsides — offside calls
  • Contact — contact calls
  • Obstruction — obstruction calls
  • Footwork — footwork calls
